## 📌 Overview

**SubTrack ₹** helps you conquer *"subscription creep"* (forgotten recurring auto-debits and scattered bills). It standardizes monthly and annual expenses into a single unified run-rate, alerts you before renewal dates, and estimates your long-term cumulative spend—all stored **100% privately in your browser's `localStorage`** with zero server tracking or sign-ups.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- ⚡ **Interactive Dashboard:** Live summary showing *Active Subscriptions Count*, *Total Monthly Spend (₹)*, *Estimated Yearly Spend (₹)*, and *Upcoming Renewal Alerts*.
- ⚖️ **Automatic Cost Normalization:** Converts annual subscriptions to monthly equivalents (`Yearly Price ÷ 12`) to uncover your true recurring burn-rate.
- 🔔 **Proactive Renewal Radar:** Chronologically sorts and highlights your nearest upcoming renewal to avoid accidental credit card / UPI e-mandate auto-debits.
- 🎬 **Animated Engine Previews:** Interactive simulation boxes demonstrating input packaging, math normalization, renewal detection, and storage vault mechanics.
- 🧮 **Waste & Savings Calculator:** Interactive sliders to project 1, 3, and 5-year cumulative commitments and estimate recoverable savings (~28% industry average waste).
- 📚 **Indian Services Directory:** Benchmark rates for top OTT, Music, AI, and Delivery services in India (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, Spotify, ChatGPT Plus, Swiggy One) with **`+ Quick Add`** functionality.
- 🛡️ **100% Client-Side Privacy:** No logins, no backend database, and zero third-party tracking. All data lives in `window.localStorage`.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive & Clean:** Modern light interface built with pure semantic HTML and plain CSS.
